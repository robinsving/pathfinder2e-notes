# Kill Vilree

**Status:** Incomplete - Vilree escaped to [[Cheliax]], but major operations dismantled

[[Vilree]] är fortfarande på fri fot. Hon lämnade ett brev med ingredienser till en frenemy i [[Cheliax]] huvudstad.

Uppdatering: Vilree har redan lyckats med att förstöra byn som förstörde hennes liv.

## Sammanfattning
Over several months (Oct-Dec 2021), the party tracked down and eliminated many of [[Vilree]]'s operations and allies, though Vilree herself escaped to [[Cheliax]]. The party discovered her hidden laboratory beneath Breachill's lumber mill, uncovered her network through investigation and combat, and battled various demons and undead forces.

- Discovers hidden alchemical laboratory

**November 11-17** - Bandit hideout operation
- Party tracks bandits to mountain grotto hideout
- Uses dynamite to seal entrances

**December 1** - Portal exploration on Hell Knight Hill
- Party explores portal system showing connections to other planes and dimensions
- Final confrontation with basilisk-like demon and demonic forces
- Battles undead skeletons and planar mechanics
